## 2026-07-28: P10C result-draft review (three independent reviewers)

**Scope.** Reviewed the P10C epoch-800 manuscript revision, its five-fold by
three-support-selection OSM-assisted PU records, the P10C configuration, and
the available production figures.

**Adopted corrections.**

- Rename every downstream split as a positive-pixel-ratio-stratified random
  patch fold, not a spatial fold or geographic holdout.
- Describe the 25 threshold-calibration patches as fully rasterized OSM masks,
  not independently labelled ground truth. Three target polygons initialise
  the reader; they are not the complete annotation cost of the experiment.
- Keep AEF as an annual-2025 contextual feature comparator, not a matched
  upstream model ranking or a global-SOTA claim.
- State that the fold-clustered intervals average three support selections
  inside each of five folds and are descriptive paired evidence, not repeated
  encoder-pretraining inference.
- Add the P10C full-region PCA only as a qualitative transductive product
  illustration; it is not a held-out result and carries no land-cover colour
  interpretation.

**Submission blockers retained.**

- The principal building, road, and water evaluation labels overlap the direct
  OSM semantic probes used upstream. Independent external or adjudicated
  labels are required for a semantic-transfer claim.
- The encoder is transductive on all 320 Haidian patches and downstream folds
  are random stratified partitions. Spatially blocked validation and a
  clean-from-scratch cross-city replication are required for generalisation
  claims.
- The AEF product is annual 2025, whereas P10C is April-indexed with a
  December 2025--May 2026 contextual window. An information-matched baseline
  is required for a model-superiority claim.
- Acquisition/compositing, quality-mask, alignment, OSM-snapshot, and
  rasterisation audits must be turned into submission-ready supplementary
  material. A study-layout figure, model workflow figure, and current-protocol
  patch-level qualitative examples are also still required.
